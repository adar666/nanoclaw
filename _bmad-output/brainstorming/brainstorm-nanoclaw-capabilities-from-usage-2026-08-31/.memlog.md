- (insight) Five real threads emerged from 76 raw ideas across 11 techniques: (1) shared-fact/context resolution across the three isolated agent groups (Yulanda/household/Tina) was independently reinvented by Ghost User Interview, One Sentence Spec (x2), and Two Bots One Brain -- the strongest, most-repeated signal, and it matches a pitfall already logged in CLAUDE.md; (2) transparency/provenance for anything automated -- receipts/why/who -- surfaced in Yes And, Infomercial, and One Sentence Spec as one idea wearing three costumes; (3) trust via visible uncertainty -- 'ask, don't guess' -- appeared in Delight Audit and One Sentence Spec, generalizing the existing guest-resolution pattern into a cross-cutting persona rule; (4) two already-shipped, proven patterns want generalizing system-wide: the calendar idempotency/duplicate-guard (JTBD) and document version-history/undo (Infomercial, One Sentence Spec); (5) persona-tuned friction -- a per-agent-group risk profile that scopes self-mod/confirmation strictness -- connects Two Bots One Brain, the $0 Mandate's reuse of the self-mod approval card, and No Rules Roadmap's capture-and-flag idea. Chosen directions to carry forward: prioritize (1) shared-fact resolution and (2) provenance/receipts as the two most buildable, highest-repeat-signal candidates for a future bmad-spec pass.
